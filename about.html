local_fire_department Our Mission

Streetball is more than just a game; it's a culture. LigaPH was born out of the need to unite hoopers across the country. We noticed how hard it was to find reliable runs, track who is actually good, and organize squads without the chaos of scattered group chats.

We are building the definitive ecosystem for players. Whether you're an elite shot-creator looking for a competitive league, or a casual hooper looking for a weekend pickup game, LigaPH puts the entire local basketball scene right in your pocket.
